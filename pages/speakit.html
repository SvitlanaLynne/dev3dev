We invite you to join our online speaking club focusing on the topics of AI, robotics and sensors. Please, email us for more details.

Format:

  • 30 min Online Meetings
  • Everyone briefly (3 min) shares just their practical findings or a business implementation of the technology.

What to expect:

  • A shared learning space — not a networking event.
  • Membership is required for participation, but there is no charge.
  • A peer-to-peer exchange — not a lecture — where we learn together.

Why Join

  • Make time to explore tools, news, basics, history, or practice around the 3 trending IT topics.
  • Practice public speaking.
  • Practice presenting under time limits.
  • Listen to diverse perspectives.
  • Share practical tools and ideas.
  • Apply discoveries to real-life projects.
